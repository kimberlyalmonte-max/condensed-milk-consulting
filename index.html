Case studies

Recent work

All work below was built inside one organization. The problems are common across mission-driven orgs. The solutions travel.

The situation

Teachers were spending planning time decoding 200-page government standards documents.

What I built

An AI chatbot — Collabby — that turns dense NYSED standards into plain-language answers for teachers working with multilingual learners. It knows the linguistic demands for ELA standards, WIDA correlations, and when to pull in bilingual resources for Spanish-speaking newcomers. Built on Claude Haiku with prompt caching, deployed on Netlify, fully branded and WCAG-accessible.

Teachers get answers in seconds instead of hunting through PDFs. Estimated cost to run: ~$18/year.
The situation

Someone was manually creating sign-in sheets for every event — the morning of.

What I built

A Google Apps Script system that queries Salesforce every night for the next day's events, generates a formatted Excel spreadsheet and a branded print-ready PDF, then emails both to the right people automatically. Temporary Sheets are created for export and immediately trashed so nothing accumulates in Drive.

Zero manual sign-in sheet creation. Zero forgotten sheets. Zero morning scramble.
The situation

Members couldn't self-serve. Staff was fielding the same "can you send me that resource?" emails on repeat.

What I built

A WordPress plugin wiring together Google OAuth, Salesforce, and Google Drive. Members sign in with their school email, see their membership tier, and browse a grade-band-filtered resource library — all without touching staff. Includes a NY State Regents transcript audit tool. Every login and download is tracked via webhook.

Self-service for 280+ member schools. Staff spends less time answering the same questions.
The situation

Zoom had the attendance data. Salesforce had the registrations. They never talked to each other.

What I built

A full Salesforce Apex event management system: automated registration and cancellation emails, real-time seat tracking rolled up to Opportunities, Zoom API integration that syncs who actually attended, and a nightly batch that catches recording viewers after the fact. Replaced manual DLRS rollups across five event types and two semesters.

One source of truth for event attendance. No more cross-referencing Zoom reports and Salesforce records by hand.
The situation

The learning platform had no idea which users were active members. Access was managed by hand.

What I built

A sync system between LearnWorlds and Salesforce running on three schedules: a daily light pass for same-day membership changes, a weekly sweep for new users, and a monthly full reconciliation. Domain-based lookup automatically matches users to their school's membership tier.

Membership changes in Salesforce reflect in the LMS within 24 hours, with no manual intervention.
The situation

Someone was scanning education news every morning and deciding what to post where — manually, every single day.

What I built

A daily automated content scan across education news sources with deduplication (so the same story never surfaces twice) and calibrated channel tagging — which items belong on Instagram, which on LinkedIn, which to drop entirely — tuned to the org's voice and mission over time.

Content decision-making is systematic, not reactive. The right stories reach the right channels without starting from scratch each morning.
